Open datasets and methods from my research. Everything below is freely available; if you use a dataset, please cite the associated paper or data record.

<div class="dataset">
<h3>Standardized Soil Moisture–Temperature Compound Index (SSTCI) — global daily compound dry–hot extremes, 1961–2023</h3>
<p>A daily-scale, global, high-resolution index for monitoring and characterising compound dry–hot (drought–heat) extremes, with an accompanying event catalogue spanning 1961–2023, designed for both scientific analysis and operational drought and heat monitoring. The method is described in the <em>International Journal of Climatology</em> (2026); the dataset and catalogue are described in a companion data paper (preprint, 2026).</p>

</div>

<div class="dataset">
<h3>Seamless FengYun-3 soil moisture record</h3>
<p>A finer-resolution, temporally consistent surface soil-moisture record built by synergistically merging the FengYun-3 passive-microwave satellite series — the outcome of a decade of retrieval improvements (open-water correction, scattering-albedo updates and correlation-preserving merging) to the Land Parameter Retrieval Model applied to China's FY-3 sensors.</p>
<div class="links"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05263-7">Data descriptor (Scientific Data, 2025)</a></div>
</div>

<p>A harmonised global land-evaporation dataset derived from model-based products, developed with Jiao Lu, Guojie Wang and colleagues.</p>

<div class="dataset">
<h3>Time-varying and multivariate Liang–Kleeman information flow</h3>
<p>Causal-inference formalisms for non-stationary climate time series: a time-varying Liang–Kleeman information flow (<em>Journal of Climate</em>, 2019) and its extension to time-dependent multivariate causal structures (<em>Journal of Climate</em>, 2024), with a recent benchmark of conditioning strategies and an application to causal importance in physics-informed machine learning (CLeaR 2026). Two patents cover algorithms developed for causality analysis and extreme-climate evaluation. Code is available on request.</p>
