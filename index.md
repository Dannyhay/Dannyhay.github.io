    <p>I am a hydro-climate research scientist working on how the land surface and the atmosphere shape one another, and on how that coupling drives droughts, heatwaves and compound dry–hot extremes. My work combines <strong>causal inference</strong> (information-theoretic methods such as the Liang–Kleeman information flow), <strong>satellite remote sensing</strong> of soil moisture and land surface temperature, <strong>land data assimilation</strong> and climate-model analysis to identify the drivers of hydroclimatic extremes and to assess what a warming climate means for food and water security.</p>

Since 2023 I have been at Ghent University's Hydro-Climate Extremes Lab, where I work on the identification of drivers of hydroclimatic extreme events, and on potential mitigation solutions, within the European Research Council project **HEAT** (*Hybrid dry–hot Extremes prediction and AdapTation*) and the FWO Belgium–China project **CausalHeat** (*Mapping the causal dynamics of dry and humid hot extremes*, 2025–2027), of which I am Co-PI. I collaborate with scientists across Europe, North America and Asia on causal frameworks for assessing changes in the climate system, and I supervise graduate students in Belgium, Germany, China, Ghana and India.

Before moving to Ghent I was a research scientist at the Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST, 2020–2023), where I developed improvement schemes for satellite soil-moisture and land-surface-temperature retrievals from passive microwave observations, contributed to NCAR's soil-moisture data-assimilation research, and developed causality frameworks for flood and drought prediction and land-induced heatwaves. In parallel I lectured at the Reading Academy (NUIST's joint institute with the University of Reading, UK). I obtained my Ph.D. (2020, with honours and an excellent-thesis award), M.Sc. and B.Sc. at NUIST, after starting my studies in geomatic engineering at the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology in Ghana, and spent time as a visiting scholar with the Data Assimilation Research Section at the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR), Boulder, USA.

Beyond the lab, I am the Early-Career Representative of the [AIMES Land Data Assimilation Working Group](https://aimesproject.org/ldawg), an external collaborator of NCAR's [DAReS](https://dart.ucar.edu/) data-assimilation group, a guest editor for *Frontiers in Environmental Science*, and an external consultant to the hydrological department of the Ghana Meteorological Agency. I also co-host the **Green Living Chats** podcast, where we talk with scientists and practitioners about sustainability and how to tackle environmental challenges from different perspectives.
