<div class="cv-section" markdown="1">
<h2>Selected achievements</h2>

- Over 80 SCI-indexed publications, including first-author papers in *Nature Communications*, *Scientific Data*, *Journal of Climate*, *Environmental Research Letters* and *Remote Sensing*.
- Two patents for algorithms for causality analysis and extreme-climate evaluation.
- Developed a time-varying (and later multivariate) information-flow causality formalism for non-stationary climate systems.
- Led, since 2015, the longest-running study of soil moisture from China's FengYun-3 passive-microwave sensors; improved LPRM and LST retrieval algorithms.
- Released open datasets: the SSTCI global daily compound dry–hot extremes dataset and event catalogue (1961–2023); merged model evapotranspiration (1981–2018); seamless FengYun-3 soil moisture.
- Leading, with NCAR's DAReS team, the development of a satellite soil-moisture assimilation scheme in the DART + CLM framework.
</div>
